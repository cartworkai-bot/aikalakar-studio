AIKALAKAR™ is the dedicated AI visual storytelling and neural cinema production brand of Cartwork Imaging, our registered creative media firm established in 2017. Our primary business and core specialization is end-to-end AI Video Production — directing, synthesizing, and delivering broadcast-grade commercial films where 100% of footage is generated using cutting-edge neural intelligence.

AIKALAKAR™ was founded in early 2023 at the inception of generative neural technology — standing as one of India's earliest independent studios dedicated entirely to cutting-edge AI cinematography, high-impact commercial advertising, and brand storytelling.

The defining advantage of AIKALAKAR™ is rooted in deep film heritage. Our founder, Yogesh (Dadarao Tupe), brings over 12+ years of hands-on experience in professional film editing, post-production direction, and managing a video production agency through Cartwork Imaging since 2017. Beyond commercial advertising, our visual work has contributed to landmark cinema productions across both Bollywood (Hindi Cinema) and Tollywood (Telugu Cinema), crafting boundary-pushing visual sequences, conceptual environments, and hyper-stylized digital assets for feature films and premier brand campaigns.

With an organic creative community of over 60,000+ (60K+) dedicated followers on Instagram (@aikalakar), our studio has become a cultural authority and visual benchmark for agencies, filmmakers, and forward-thinking enterprises across India.
